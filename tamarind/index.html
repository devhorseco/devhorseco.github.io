Demo created by DevHorse as a proposal for Tamarind Divers. It only uses what Tamarind shared: half-day trips to Nusa Penida, a simple local dish with rice and vegetables on board (no restaurant or menu), PADI courses up to Divemaster and the Mola Mola Awareness specialty. The meeting time is a blank for Tamarind to fill in, dietary adjustments are marked “to confirm”, the packing list is a suggestion Tamarind can edit, and the diver name and lunch tally are samples.
